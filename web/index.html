The difference

Five things most firms will not put in writing.

We name the engineers.

You meet the squad before you sign, and the names on the proposal are the names in the standups.

We publish the numbers.

Lead time, release cadence and uptime go in the monthly report whether or not they flatter us.

We stay past launch.

Most of our work is month thirteen onward. Launch is the middle of an engagement, not the end.

We tell you what went wrong.

Every retro that reaches you includes the things we got wrong and what changed afterwards.

Your data stays in Canada.

Client data is held in Canadian regions. Named regions go here once confirmed.

How we work

Three phases, because there are only three.

The tagline is the process. Competitors run five to seven phases because it fills a slide.

01

Design

Two to four weeks. We scope with the engineers who will build it, so the estimate comes from the people held to it.

02

Develop

Two-week increments, demoed to you, shipped behind flags. You see working software in week three, not a status deck.

03

Refine

The squad stays. Lead time comes down, the backlog gets shorter, and the same people know why every decision was made.
