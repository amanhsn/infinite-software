One dark band, and the rest is paper.

The reference spec and the brand book disagreed on theme, accent and typeface. The book wins — it is the source of truth. What survives from the spec is its craft: the glass, the radii, the grain, the easing. All of it concentrates in one ink band, which is the only dark surface on the site and the only place the brand permits it.

Two brand rules read as AI tells but are not. Uppercase tracked eyebrows and a mono face for figures are both written specifications in the book (Figtree 700 +0.2em; Plex Mono 500 tabular). They stay because the brand mandates them, not because they are defaults.

What I will not invent

Six of the fourteen sections need data only you have. I will build them as marked slots with realistic structure, never with fabricated clients, logos, metrics or quotes.
