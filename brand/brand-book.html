Construction

The centreline is a Gerono lemniscate, defined by two numbers. Anyone can redraw the mark exactly — there is no tracing and no drift between versions.

y(t) = (b/2) · sin 2t

Logo

Clear space

Leave room around the logo equal to half the mark's height — 65 units in the artwork. Nothing enters it: no type, no rule, no photograph edge.

x = 65 units = half the mark height. The dashed field is a minimum, not a target.

Logo

One ramp, two states

A single left-to-right ramp runs across the whole mark, and both halves sample it at the same point — so the colour never breaks at the join. The receding half is that same ramp lifted toward white as the surface turns away.

Front — full strength, sampled across the mark's width
Back — the same ramp, lifted 62% toward white at the tip, 26% at the crossing

The only tonal step left is at the crossing, which is exactly where one surface passes in front of another. Remove it and the twist reads flat.

Co-branding

When Infinite appears beside a client or partner mark, both sit on a shared optical centreline with a hairline rule between them. Infinite never leads on a client's own material.

Client
Partner lockup · equal weight
Delivered by
Endorsement · Infinite in support

Clear space applies to both marks independently. Never scale one to match the other's bounding box — match optical weight instead.

The gradient system

Two gradients, one palette. The light mesh rises out of white and sits behind content. The frame mesh is evenly lit and carries the logo alone.

Light mesh — hero panels, covers, page grounds
Frame mesh — logo frames, app icons, social
Paper & mist~80% of any surface
Ink~15%
Cobalt~4%
Gradient~1%

If the gradient is on more than one surface in a room, it has stopped being special.

Greys and states
