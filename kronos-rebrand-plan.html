Rebrand plan · for approval

Kronos Labs

Two K monograms, one dark identity in the neighbourhood of HiddenLayer, and a green pulled back toward NVIDIA. This is the plan — nothing is built yet. Once you pick a route and answer the four questions at the end, I build it the same way as the Infinite and Echo books: a paginated landscape manual generated from a script, fonts inlined, prints to PDF.

01

What you gave me

Both files are a single compound path carrying the monogram and the stacked wordmark together. I split them on subpath geometry, so the mark can stand alone — the artwork itself is untouched and will be used exactly as supplied.

Angular

Hard 45° chevrons, one rounded outer corner. Engineered, defence-adjacent, closest in temperament to the security category.

Petal

The same arms swept into leaf forms. Organic and warmer — reads research lab or life sciences more than threat intelligence.

03

The green

NVIDIA's #76B900 is a dark yellow-green built for white backgrounds — dropped straight onto a near-black ground it goes muddy and loses the glow that makes HiddenLayer's accent work. So I held NVIDIA's hue (82°) and raised the luminance until it matched HiddenLayer's brightness. That is the first swatch: same family as NVIDIA, same behaviour as the reference.

05

What I need from you

Which K ships

Angular and Petal are the same monogram at different temperatures. Angular reads engineered and defence-adjacent; Petal reads organic and softer, closer to a life-sciences or research lab. I can build the book on one, or on one with the other recorded as an alternate.

Wordmark

Both files carry the same stacked Kronos Labs in a heavy geometric grotesque. I can keep it verbatim as supplied, or reset it in the chosen brand face so the lockup and the headlines agree.

How dark

HiddenLayer commits fully to dark — there is no light mode on the marketing site. Same for Kronos, or a light ground for documents and decks with dark reserved for the site?

Typeface budget

HiddenLayer uses Degular, which is licensed. If Kronos will pay for a face I will spec one; otherwise I will pick the best open-licensed match and inline it, as with the other two books.
